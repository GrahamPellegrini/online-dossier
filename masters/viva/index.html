MSc AI Dissertation Viva11 | Method | Posterior collapse

Posterior collapse

Diffusion Policy denoises Gaussian noise into a motion over DDIM steps. 5 independent noise samples give 5 candidates.

ACT is a CVAE, where a latent z sampled at test time should vary the motion. Posterior collapse means the decoder learned to ignore z, so all 5 samples are the same motion.

MSc AI Dissertation Viva13 | Method | CEMP

CEMP: Cross-Entropy Method with Policy

Cross-entropy method, started from the policy's output (inspired by TD-MPC2).

The same world model builds the pool and then picks from it, so the pool already leans towards what that world model prefers.

ACT results therefore measure the world model and the planner as one unit.

MSc AI Dissertation Viva14 | Method | Adaptive rule

CEMP only applied where needed

Only seed 42 needed CEMP, so seeds 43 and 44 were scored on ACT's own candidates. The pooled ACT result therefore mixes a world-model-plus-planner measurement with a plain scorer measurement. Read it as one result for this rule, not as a clean measurement of the scorer. This is why Diffusion Policy carries the main claim.

MSc AI Dissertation Viva19 | Interpretation | Three conditions

What a world model needs to help

  • Diverse poolThe candidates must be genuinely different motions, or there is nothing to choose between.
  • Action informationThe score must depend on each candidate's motion. Without it, every score ties.
  • In-support candidatesCandidates must be close to the scorer's training data, so its predictions can be trusted.

MSc AI Dissertation VivaBackup G | Metrics

How error is measured

Translation error = average, over the 16 steps and both wrists, of the root-mean-square position error per axis (metres)

Gain = mean error of the chosen candidate ÷ mean error of candidate 0, minus 1

Rotation error is the same idea for orientation: the mean geodesic angle in degrees. Error is always measured against the recorded human motion, which the world model never sees.
