MSc AI Dissertation Viva19 | Comparison | DiWA's design
DiWA, Chandra et al. (2025)

DiWA meets all three conditions by design

  1. Diverse poolThe policy samples and explores freely in imagination.
  2. Action informationEvery imagined step is driven by the policy's own action.
  3. In supportReward and update come from the same rollouts, so they cannot drift apart.

The loop runs offline inside the frozen world model; the finetuned policy is then evaluated in CALVIN. Point 3 follows the paper's own Appendix S.4.5.

MSc AI Dissertation Viva20 | Comparison | Our selection
Our external selection

Our selection meets them only when the pool and scorer allow

  1. Diverse poolMet for Diffusion Policy. ACT collapsed to five copies.
  2. Action informationV-JEPA2-AC reads the motion. Remove it and the gain is 0.00%.
  3. In supportThe scorer is trained apart from the policy. Adapter candidates fell outside it.

Complementary, not competing: finetune when an environment-like world model and a reward exist, select when only recorded data does.

MSc AI Dissertation VivaBackup G | Metrics and statistics

How results were measured

Error metrics
  • Translation, root-mean-square per axis, not Euclidean distance
  • Rotation, mean geodesic angle in degrees
  • Both averaged over the full 16-step chunk
  • Change reported relative to candidate 0
Statistics
  • 95% episode-clustered bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, pooled over seeds
  • Oracle ceiling, best candidate in the same pool
  • Ranking-validity gate, per-window Spearman correlation
  • Win, tie, loss against candidate 0

Test windows are fixed, candidate pools frozen, future-goal access blocked, and scorer support restricted to training data.

MSc AI Dissertation VivaBackup H | Test-set handling

What is disclosed about test-set use

  • The Branch 2 margin threshold's final value came from a disjoint validation split.
  • An earlier, superseded calibration touched the test set directly. This is stated as a named limitation and is reassuring, not a full defence.
  • The LeWM gate was tuned on a small diagnostic split and failed to generalise to the full test set. It was reported, not re-tuned.
  • Earlier stronger-looking results relying on shortcuts, transductive support or oracle access were corrected rather than reported.
