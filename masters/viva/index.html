MSc AI Dissertation Viva16 | Interpretation

Selection helped when three conditions held together

1 Diverse pool

There must be real alternatives to choose between. ACT's collapsed pool offered none until search restored it.

2 Action information

The scorer must see the action. Removing it gave exactly 0.00%.

3 In-support candidates

Candidates must lie within what the scorer was trained on. Adapter pilots improved a latent score and not the trajectory.

Why finetuning wins

DiWA's imagined-PPO finetuning meets all three conditions by construction, because its reward signal is what the policy is trained against. External selection has no such guarantee.

This is a pattern supported by the tested configurations, not a general law.

MSc AI Dissertation Viva17 | Limits and next steps

What this does and does not show

Limits
  • Offline trajectory error, not robot task success
  • One dataset and one positive pairing
  • Matched exposure, not equal pretraining
  • LeWM's gain is unexplained
  • Selection worsens the easiest fifth of windows
Next steps
  • Closed-loop execution, starting with CALVIN-style tasks
  • A second, independent world model as selector over a finetuned policy
  • Native 18-D finetuning of DINO-WM and JEPA-WM
  • DexWM at its published scale
  • A diversity mechanism for ACT

Contribution in one sentence. A controlled offline benchmark and protocol showing a frozen world model can improve which of a policy's own proposed motions is chosen, for one tested pairing, without retraining and without any claim yet about real-robot success.

MSc AI Dissertation VivaBackup H | Metrics and statistics

How results were measured

Error metrics
  • Translation, root-mean-square per axis, not Euclidean distance
  • Rotation, mean geodesic angle in degrees
  • Both averaged over the full 16-step chunk
  • Change reported relative to candidate 0
Statistics
  • 95% episode-clustered bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, pooled over seeds
  • Oracle ceiling, best candidate in the same pool
  • Ranking-validity gate, per-window Spearman correlation
  • Win, tie, loss against candidate 0

Test windows are fixed, candidate pools frozen, future-goal access blocked, and scorer support restricted to training data.

MSc AI Dissertation VivaBackup I | Test-set handling

What is disclosed about test-set use

  • The Branch 2 margin threshold's final value came from a disjoint validation split.
  • An earlier, superseded calibration touched the test set directly. This is stated as a named limitation and is reassuring, not a full defence.
  • The LeWM gate was tuned on a small diagnostic split and failed to generalise to the full test set. It was reported, not re-tuned.
  • Earlier stronger-looking results relying on shortcuts, transductive support or oracle access were corrected rather than reported.
