Does the score order match the true order?

Spearman rank correlation: how well the world model's ranking of the candidates matches their true ranking. Gain says how much error drops; correlation says whether the ranking is right.

V-JEPA2-AC: per window, candidate scores against candidate errors (K = 5, pooled seeds). LeWM: across windows, score margin against true improvement (+0.54 on a 512-window check, -0.51 on all 7,607). The no-action control ties every score.
