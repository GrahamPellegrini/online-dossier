Getting started with AI for Work and Study

DGP0959 · Student setup guide · Graham Pellegrini

You can take part without buying an AI subscription. Start with a tool you can access; if access fails, use the provided example response or work with a partner. Optional benefits below have their own eligibility conditions.

1. Check your access

Bring a Wi-Fi laptop or tablet. Check that you can open the VLE course area and your UM email. If you have not activated your UM account, follow the UM account guidance or contact UM IT Services. Tell the tutor if you cannot access the VLE; do not create a second university identity to work around a problem.

Before class, record privately: which tool you can access, whether your account is personal or university-managed, and whether you can start a chat. Keep passwords and verification codes private.

2. Start with Gemini through your UM account

UM IT Services confirms Gemini access for staff and students, including research and custom-assistant features, with usage caps. UM-account interactions are not used to train Google's models or reviewed by humans under UM's agreement. This does not grant permission to upload confidential material. See UM Google Workspace guidance.

  1. Open Gemini in your browser.
  2. Sign in using your activated UM account through the institutional sign-in flow.
  3. Check the account indicator to ensure you are using your UM identity, rather than a personal Google account.
  4. Start a chat and try the fictional exercise below.
  5. If access is denied or a feature is missing, note the message and contact IT Services. Use the class fallback meanwhile.

A university email does not automatically confer every paid consumer Google plan. Check what your account actually offers. Individual participant access still needs to be checked in class.

3. Start with ChatGPT

Six steps to your first conversation:

  1. Open ChatGPT. Go to chatgpt.com in your browser.
  2. Sign in or create an account. Use your existing login, or follow the account-creation option on the site. Complete any verification it asks for. Ask the tutor if you get stuck.
  3. Read the terms. Open the European Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Read the terms shown during signup too. You can start our exercise without buying an upgrade.
  4. Start a chat. Open a new conversation in ChatGPT. If you see a choice between Chat and Work, choose Chat. The official quickstart explains the browser steps.
  5. Try the first task. Copy the short prompt below, send it, and read the answer. Type a follow-up in the same chat to change the result.
  6. Keep what helps. Copy the useful answer into your notes. Save your prompt and your own corrections beside it.

Need help? The screens may look slightly different on your device. Show the tutor the problem without sharing your password or verification code. You can use a saved example while access is sorted out.

4. Try a first task and check it

Start with three fictional notes. No upload or timetable is needed.

Bring my laptop on Wednesday. Read the handout before class. Write down one question to ask.

Copy this prompt:

Turn these three notes into a short checklist. Use only the information in the notes: Bring my laptop on Wednesday. Read the handout before class. Write down one question to ask.

Check the answer: Are all three tasks included? Has it added a date, deadline or task that was not in the notes?

Then ask for one change:

Make each checklist item shorter. Keep the same three tasks.

You have now given an instruction, checked a result and asked for a revision. Keep the wording you find useful.

5. Optional parallel learning: AI għal Kulħadd

AI for All is a free national learning programme for Maltese citizens and residents with an active eID. Successful completion can provide a certificate and a 12-month AI-platform subscription. This is separate from DegreePlus attendance.

  1. Follow the official service page to ai4all.gov.mt.
  2. Register/sign in with eID; choose a language and complete onboarding.
  3. Under My Catalogues, activate the AI Programme Bundle and open it from My Bundles.
  4. Follow Access Course, complete the three fundamental modules, then the platform exam.
  5. When eligible, choose one provider: ChatGPT Plus or Microsoft 365 Personal with Copilot AI. The selection cannot change after a code is issued.
  6. Follow your personal redemption instructions. A ChatGPT code requires no active paid ChatGPT subscription at redemption; it must be redeemed within 12 months of 18 May 2026.
  7. Keep the code private. Use the programme's Help & Support if blocked.

See the programme FAQ and ChatGPT redemption page for current terms. Do not cancel or purchase a subscription solely on the basis of this guide; check your own eligibility and instructions first.

Recommended pace: start after Session 1 and complete alongside the early workshops, at a pace that suits you. Students without eID or an eligible subscription remain fully included in DGP0959.

6. Use AI in coursework with permission

Check your assignment's instructions and your tutor's permission before using AI for assessed answers. Retain drafts, check sources, and follow the required acknowledgement rules. The UM student FAQ distinguishes learning support from assessed work.

For our practice, use supplied fictional examples or public material. A practice exercise here is not permission to generate an assessed report in another unit.

Keep a simple learning log: task; tool and displayed model/version if available; prompt; useful output; checks; your edits. This helps you explain how you worked.

7. Before Session 1

  • Bring your device and charger.
  • Try one available tool and the example above.
  • Note one question and one task you want to improve.
  • Tell the tutor privately about access difficulties.

Interface screenshots will be added after a tutor checks the actual accounts and screens. This guide collects no account details and is not a login form.
