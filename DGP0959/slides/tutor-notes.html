Session 1: Your AI Toolkit — tutor and revision notes

Graham Pellegrini · DGP0959 · 14 October 2026 · ELT · 12:00–14:00

These notes accompany the opening slides currently under review. They are suitable for speaking preparation and for students revising after class. Further sections will be added as each slide is reviewed.

1. Introduction: Your AI Toolkit

Slide purpose: Welcome students and establish that the course is practical and suitable for beginners.

Suggested explanation: We will practise everyday study and work tasks with AI tools. You do not need programming skills or previous AI experience. The aim is to choose a useful tool, give it clear instructions, inspect its response and improve it. You remain responsible for the result.

Ask the class: What is one study or work task you would like help with? A short answer is enough. Account access and experience will be checked in the opening diagnostic later.

For revision: AI assistance is a starting point for your work. A fluent response still needs checking. You can participate using the provided examples if tool access is unavailable.

2. Course overview

Slide purpose: Show the complete eight-session journey, with dates, topics and a short description for each.

Suggested explanation: We begin with choosing tools and giving instructions. We then practise document work, communication, planning, presentations and simple data questions. The final session connects these skills into a repeatable workflow relevant to your own field.

Walk through the rows briefly. Use one familiar example, such as turning notes into a checklist, to show what practical learning means. The detailed workshop roadmap provides further context.
