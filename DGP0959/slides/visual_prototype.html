DGP0959 | Session 01: AI ToolkitTool Choice

AI tools are useful when the task is clear

The first decision is not the prompt. It is whether the task needs exploration, drafting, comparison, planning, or verification.

Explore

Use AI to understand a topic, list options, or generate questions before research.

Draft

Use AI to turn notes into a first version that you can check and improve.

Verify

Use reliable sources and human judgement before trusting important claims.

DGP0959 | Session 03: DocumentsReusable Prompt

Prompt pattern: summarise without losing decisions

Summarise this document for a student who needs to act on it. Return: 1. the main point; 2. any dates or deadlines; 3. decisions already made; 4. actions I need to take; 5. claims I should verify.

Prompt slides should remain readable in the PDF, so long prompts are split into clear numbered lines.

DGP0959 | Session 01: AI ToolkitPrivacy

Check before pasting

Do not paste private, sensitive, client, patient, student, or unpublished university information into an AI tool unless you know it is allowed.

Remove names, IDs, and contact details.
Use fictional examples when possible.
Ask for structure, not for decisions you must own.
When in doubt, stop and check policy.

DGP0959 | Session 02: Better ResultsComparison

Compare outputs before accepting one

Accept first answer

  • May miss your audience.
  • May hide weak assumptions.
  • Often sounds generic.
  • Harder to trust later.

Ask for alternatives

  • Shows different structures.
  • Makes trade-offs visible.
  • Lets you combine the best parts.
  • Keeps you in control.

DGP0959 | Session 08: WorkflowRecap

What to take away

1

Choose the tool by task: explore, draft, compare, plan, present, or verify.

2

Good prompts give purpose, context, audience, constraints, and format.

3

The final judgement stays with you, especially for private or important work.
