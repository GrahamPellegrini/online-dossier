DGP0959 | Better ResultsPrompt console

Prompt like you are briefing a colleague

Prompt
Draft a polite email to my lecturer. Context: I missed today's class due to illness. Goal: ask what I should review before next week. Tone: concise and professional.
Why it works
The AI has a role, context, goal, audience, and tone. The output is easier to judge and revise.
