A stronger prompt gives context

Prompt pattern: extract decisions

Summarise this document for a student who needs to act on it. Return: 1. main point; 2. dates or deadlines; 3. decisions already made; 4. actions I need to take; 5. claims I should verify.

DGP0959 | Session 05: PlanningActivity

Turn a messy goal into an action plan

Hands-on activity
Choose one real assignment, project, or student task.
Ask AI to break it into milestones, actions, and risks.
Revise the result so every action has a clear next step.
Mark which parts require human judgement.

DGP0959 | Session 01: AI ToolkitPrivacy

Check before pasting

Do not paste private, sensitive, client, patient, student, or unpublished university information into an AI tool unless you know it is allowed.

Remove names, IDs, and contact details.
Use fictional examples when possible.
When in doubt, stop and check policy.

DGP0959 | Session 08: WorkflowRecap

What to take away

1

Choose the tool by task: explore, draft, compare, plan, present, or verify.

2

Good prompts include purpose, context, audience, constraints, and format.

3

The final judgement stays with you, especially for private or important work.
